83 Argo profiles · north-west Atlantic · June 2023 · public data, no OceanX data used. Colour is temperature; how solid something is drawn is how much measurement stands behind it. Argo via Ifremer ERDDAP · GEBCO_2021 · layer after Feucher et al. 2019, mapped by objective analysis.
Argo (Ifremer ERDDAP), ascending casts, QC flags 1–2, 5-dbar bins. Field between casts: anisotropic Gaussian kernel, Lh = 25 km, Lv = 30 dbar. “Support” = normalised kernel weight, a geometry-only proxy, not a formal error. Iso-surface and slices are cut from that same field, so all are limited by that kernel. The pycnocline volume is not: it is the permanent (main) pycnocline, located in each cast's own 5-dbar N² profile by the order of the stratification extrema after Feucher et al. (2016, 2019) — seasonal peak, mode-water minimum, then the deep peak below it — with its top and base where N² falls to the slider's fraction of that peak. The two depths are mapped between casts by objective analysis (Gauss-Markov), each on its own covariance cross-validated over the casts, and each sheet is shaded by that analysis's normalised error variance rather than by the kernel-weight proxy — scaled so that it reports the error the map is measured to make at a held-out cast (7.2× on the base, 3.3× on the top), which is why it fades out where the casts are not. Two casts have no such structure at all and are dropped rather than forced. Timeline on: casts also weighted exp(−Δt²/2Lt²), two-sided (a completed record, so the estimate at an instant uses casts either side of it), Lt fitted from these casts. The uncertainty-weight slider suppresses that encoding for comparison; below 1 the render overstates what was measured and says so in the badge.
